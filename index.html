Our Story

Brewed with passion, served like family

Bite & Brew began with one simple belief — a cafe should feel like a second home. What started as a small counter with a single espresso machine has grown into a warm corner where friends meet, families celebrate and every guest is greeted by name.

From our wood-fired oven to our slow-brewed coffee, everything on the menu is made fresh, in-house, every single day.

Abhijit Awatare

Founder & Owner
